# Lantern & Lullaby — Narrative Bible

## Part 1: The Arrival (Intro Cinematic)

### Scene: Moving Day

*Visual: A car drives through unfamiliar streets. Rain streaks the window. Through the glass, we see a small town — old buildings, autumn leaves, gray sky.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
Moving is hard.

*Visual: The car passes a school. Children play in the yard. The MC watches from the backseat, face half-reflected in the glass.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
Everything you knew is somewhere else now. Your friends. Your room. The way the light came through your window in the morning.

*Visual: The car turns onto a quiet street. Old houses with porches. A church steeple in the distance.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
Your parents say you'll make new friends. That you'll love it here eventually. That change is good for you.

*Visual: The car stops. A moving truck is already parked outside a modest house. Boxes everywhere.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
They mean well. But they don't understand.

*Visual: The MC steps out. Looks around. Pulls their jacket tighter. The town feels vast and empty.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
When you're young, "eventually" feels like forever.

*Visual: The MC walks alone down the sidewalk while parents deal with movers. Exploring. Lost.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
So you walk. Because walking is better than unpacking. Better than pretending you're okay.

*Visual: The MC turns a corner. Stops. Looks up.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
And sometimes, when you're not looking for anything at all...

*Visual: A narrow alley between buildings. At the end, a small shop. Hand-painted sign, faded with age: **PAGES & EMBERS — Books & Curiosities***

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
...you find exactly what you need.

## Part 2: The Bookstore (Interactive Scene)

### Scene: Entering Pages & Embers

*The door creaks. A bell chimes — but the sound is strange. Almost musical. Like it's welcoming you specifically.*

*The shop is cramped and wonderful. Bookshelves reach to the ceiling, stuffed with volumes of every size. Dust motes drift in the light from a single window. The smell is old paper, candle wax, and something else — something that reminds you of campfires and distant thunder.*

*No one seems to be here.*

**MC (internal)**
Hello? Is anyone...

*A voice, warm and slightly amused, comes from somewhere deeper in the shop:*

**SHOPKEEPER (O.S.)**
I'm always here. Though "here" is somewhat flexible.

*An old man emerges from between the shelves. He's tall and thin, with a long white beard and eyes that seem to hold secrets. He wears a rumpled cardigan over what might be — are those robes?*

*He studies you for a long moment. Then smiles.*

**SHOPKEEPER**
Ah. There you are. I was beginning to wonder.

**MC**
I'm sorry, I was just—

**SHOPKEEPER**
Looking. Yes. That's what you do best, isn't it? Looking at things others walk past. Wondering about things others ignore.

*He gestures around the shop.*

**SHOPKEEPER**
Most people walk by this place a thousand times and never see it. But you saw it on your first day.

*He chuckles.*

**SHOPKEEPER**
That's not an accident.

*The shopkeeper moves toward a particular shelf. His fingers brush spines as he walks, and you'd swear the books lean toward his touch.*

**SHOPKEEPER**
You're new here. I can tell. You have that look — the one that says "I don't belong anywhere yet."

*He pulls a book from the shelf. It's old. Leather-bound. The cover shows a sword thrust into a stone, surrounded by trees and stars.*

**SHOPKEEPER**
But here's a secret, young one: *nobody* belongs anywhere at first. Belonging is something you build. Story by story. Dream by dream.

*He holds out the book.*

**SHOPKEEPER**
This one's been waiting for you. Has been for quite some time.

**MC**
Waiting? Books don't wait.

**SHOPKEEPER**
*amused*
Don't they?

*You take the book. It's heavier than it looks. And warm — like it's been sitting in sunlight, though this corner of the shop is dim.*

*The title, embossed in faded gold:*

**THE ONCE AND FUTURE KING**

*The shopkeeper reaches behind the counter and produces something else: a small brass lantern. Old-fashioned. Beautiful. A candle waits inside, unlit.*

**SHOPKEEPER**
You'll need this too.

**MC**
What for?

**SHOPKEEPER**
For finding your way. Dreams can be dark places, and some books... well. Some books are deeper than others.

*You take the lantern. The moment your fingers close around it, the flame sparks to life — by itself. You gasp.*

*The shopkeeper doesn't seem surprised.*

**SHOPKEEPER**
Ah. Good. It likes you.

*Outside the window, the light changes. The afternoon sun dims. Clouds roll in with unnatural speed. The first rumble of thunder shakes the windowpanes.*

*The shopkeeper glances at the storm, then back at you. His expression is kind but serious.*

**SHOPKEEPER**
You should go home now. Read the first chapter before bed tonight. Light the lantern when you do.

*He pauses.*

**SHOPKEEPER**
And remember: in dreams, nothing is quite what it seems. Not even you.

**MC**
What does that—

**SHOPKEEPER**
Off you go. Your parents will worry.

*He turns and disappears between the shelves. When you blink, he's gone — vanished, as if he were never there.*

*Thunder rolls again. The lantern glows steadily in your hand.*

*You run home through the rain, the book clutched to your chest.*

## Part 3: The First Dream (Book Opening Transition)

### Scene: Bedroom, Night

*The storm has passed. Your new room is full of boxes you haven't unpacked. Unfamiliar shadows. Strange shapes.*

*But the book sits on your pillow. The lantern glows on your nightstand.*

*You can't explain it, but you feel like the book is waiting.*

*You open to the first page.*

---

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
*Text appears as if being written:*

In the beginning, there was a boy who didn't know he was a king.

His name was Wart. He was small and overlooked and thought he would never be anyone special.

He was wrong.

*The words begin to glow. The lantern flickers. The light fills your vision.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
This is his story. But now, it's also yours.

*The light grows brighter. The room fades. You're falling — no, floating — through pages and starlight.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
Close your eyes.

Open them in the dream.

*FLASH*

---

### Scene: The Dream Begins

*You stand in a moonlit forest. Medieval. Ancient. The air smells of pine and woodsmoke.*

*You look down. You're dressed differently — simple clothes, a tunic. No phone, no sneakers. Just you.*

*The lantern hangs at your belt. Its flame burns low but steady.*

*A voice — familiar now, but younger somehow — calls from ahead:*

**WART (O.S.)**
Hey! Over here!

*A boy your age waves from the tree line. Scruffy. Earnest. Eyes full of wonder.*

**WART**
I don't know how you got here, but I'm glad you did. Something's wrong in the forest tonight. The wolves are acting strange.

*He grins nervously.*

**WART**
Merlyn says I need to learn to be brave. I think this might be my chance.

*He looks at your lantern.*

**WART**
That's beautiful. Does it really light the way?

**WART**
Well. There's only one way to find out.

*He gestures into the dark forest.*

**WART**
Together?

## Part 4: Post-Battle Dialogue (First Win)

### Scene: Forest Clearing, After Victory

*The wolves scatter. The forest breathes again. Wart stares at you, wide-eyed.*

**WART**
That was... you were...

*He laughs — half relief, half disbelief.*

**WART**
I've never seen anyone fight like that. Merlyn always says I'll learn, but he makes me turn into animals and learn lessons about government. You just...

*He shakes his head.*

**WART**
You just knew what to do.

*You look at your hands. The lantern pulses gently.*

**MC (internal)**
Did I? It felt like the book was... guiding me.

**WART**
Maybe we could train together? I know you're not from here. I can tell. But that doesn't matter.

*He extends his hand.*

**WART**
I'm Arthur. Well, everyone calls me Wart. But my real name is Arthur.

*Beat.*

**WART**
Don't tell anyone though. Kay would never let me hear the end of it.

*You shake his hand. The world shimmers. The lantern flickers.*

**WART**
*noticing*
Your light's getting dim. What does that mean?

*The forest starts to fade at the edges. Colors bleed away.*

**MC (internal)**
I'm... waking up?

**WART**
Wait! Will you come back? Tomorrow night?

*You try to answer, but the light swallows everything.*

---

### Scene: Bedroom, Morning

*You wake with a gasp.*

*Sunlight streams through unfamiliar curtains. For a moment, you don't know where you are.*

*Then you remember. The new town. The new house. The bookstore.*

*The dream.*

*You look at your hand. No lantern. But when you turn your head, there it is on the nightstand — flame extinguished, metal cool.*

*The book lies open on your chest. The page you fell asleep on has changed. There's an illustration now that wasn't there before — or was it? A boy with a sword. Wolves. And a figure in the background that might be... you.*

**MC (internal)**
It was just a dream.

*You close the book. Stare at the ceiling.*

**MC (internal)**
...wasn't it?

*Your mom calls from downstairs: breakfast, first day of school, hurry up.*

*You swing your legs out of bed. But you pause at the door, looking back at the book and lantern.*

*Tonight. You'll read more tonight.*

*You're not sure if you're excited or scared.*

*Maybe both.*

## Part 5: Hero Introduction Dialogues

### Lancelot (Chapter 1, Stage 5)

*After the battle, a knight steps from the shadows. He moves like water — graceful, dangerous, beautiful.*

**LANCELOT**
You fight well, young dreamer. Better than most knights I've known.

*He removes his helm. His face is handsome but sad. Eyes that have seen too much.*

**LANCELOT**
I am Lancelot du Lac. The finest knight in Arthur's service.

*He pauses.*

**LANCELOT**
At least... that's what they say. Whether I deserve the title is another matter.

*He kneels.*

**LANCELOT**
But I see something in you. A flame that refuses to go out. I would follow that flame, if you'll have me.

**WART**
*whispered*
Lancelot? THE Lancelot? He's even more impressive than Merlyn's stories!

**LANCELOT**
*with a slight smile*
Merlyn is generous with his stories. And not always accurate.

*He rises.*

**LANCELOT**
Shall we continue? The forest grows darker ahead.

---

### Kay (Chapter 1, Stage 8)

*Kay emerges from a failed ambush, sword at your throat — then stops.*

**KAY**
Wait. You're not one of them.

*He lowers his weapon. Scowls.*

**KAY**
You're with Wart? Of course you are. That boy attracts trouble like honey attracts flies.

**WART**
Kay! You're okay!

**KAY**
*grudgingly*
No thanks to you, running off into cursed forests.

*He looks at you.*

**KAY**
I'm his brother. Well, foster brother. I'm also the better fighter, the better hunter, and the better everything, despite what anyone tells you.

**LANCELOT**
*drily*
Humility isn't your gift, I see.

**KAY**
Humility is for people who have things to be humble about.

*Despite his bluster, there's a flicker of something else in his eyes when he looks at Wart. Protectiveness.*

**KAY**
But fine. If you're keeping this idiot alive, I suppose I'll help.

*He falls into step beside you.*

**KAY**
*muttering*
Someone has to make sure the dreamer gets home in one piece.

---

### Bedivere (Chapter 2, Stage 3)

*The one-armed knight blocks the gate alone. His stance is iron. His eyes are grief.*

**BEDIVERE**
Hold.

*He studies you. Takes in Wart, Lancelot, Kay. The lantern at your belt.*

**BEDIVERE**
You're not Morgause's creatures. And that light... I haven't seen its like in many years.

*He lowers his shield.*

**BEDIVERE**
I am Bedivere. The king's man. The last of his original companions.

*He raises the stump of his right arm.*

**BEDIVERE**
I gave my arm for Arthur. I would give the rest if duty demanded.

**LANCELOT**
*soft*
Old friend. It's been too long.

**BEDIVERE**
Lancelot.

*A beat. Something unspoken passes between them.*

**BEDIVERE**
I thought you'd abandoned us. After... everything.

**LANCELOT**
I could never abandon the king. Only... step aside when my presence did more harm than good.

*Bedivere nods slowly. Turns to you.*

**BEDIVERE**
The dreamer leads this company?

**WART**
They do. I trust them.

**BEDIVERE**
*after a long moment*
Then so do I. One-armed or not, my shield is yours.

---

### Morgana (Chapter 2, Stage 8)

*She steps from shadow itself. Beautiful. Terrifying. Neither wholly good nor wholly evil.*

**MORGANA**
You've come far, little dreamer. Farther than most who enter these pages.

*Her eyes — green as envy, green as spring — study you.*

**MORGANA**
I am Morgana le Fay. Sister to the king. Daughter of Igraine. Called witch by those who fear me. Called worse by those who should know better.

**KAY**
She's dangerous. Don't trust her.

**MORGANA**
*amused*
The seneschal's boy, warning others about trust. Delicious.

*She circles you. The lantern flickers but doesn't dim.*

**MORGANA**
I have been many things. Villain. Victim. Villain again. The stories change depending on who tells them.

*She stops.*

**MORGANA**
But I'm tired of being what others expect. Your light... it doesn't judge. It simply illuminates.

*She extends her hand. Dark power crackles at her fingertips — then softens. Becomes starlight.*

**MORGANA**
Let me show you what I could be. If someone believed in me.

---

### Escanor (Chapter 3, Stage 5)

*The massive knight bursts from the prison tower like a sun breaking through clouds. Chains trail from his wrists. He flexes, and they shatter.*

**ESCANOR**
FINALLY!

*His voice booms across the courtyard. Turquine's remaining guards flee.*

**ESCANOR**
Sixty knights he imprisoned! SIXTY! And none with the courage to face him!

*He sees you. Squints.*

**ESCANOR**
But you... you are small. Why would one so small dare Turquine's castle?

*Before you can answer, Wart speaks up:*

**WART**
They came to help. They always help.

*Escanor studies you. His expression shifts from contempt to curiosity to something like respect.*

**ESCANOR**
Hmm. Bravery is not measured in inches.

*He laughs — a sound like thunder.*

**ESCANOR**
I am Escanor! My strength grows with the sun and peaks at noon! Normally I fight alone, for no one can match my power!

*He grins.*

**ESCANOR**
But you have earned my respect, tiny dreamer. I will GRACE this company with my magnificence!

**KAY**
*under his breath*
Great. Another humble one.

---

### Galahad (Chapter 3, Stage 8)

*The young knight kneels before an altar of light. He is young — perhaps only a few years older than you — but his presence fills the chapel.*

*He turns. His face is serene. Almost unbearably pure.*

**GALAHAD**
The light told me you would come.

*He stands. Moves toward you. His armor gleams with an inner radiance.*

**GALAHAD**
I am Galahad. Son of Lancelot. Seeker of the Holy Grail.

*Lancelot steps forward. His voice breaks.*

**LANCELOT**
My... my son.

**GALAHAD**
*gently*
Father.

*A moment passes. Galahad places a hand on Lancelot's shoulder.*

**GALAHAD**
Your sins are known to me. And forgiven.

*He turns to you.*

**GALAHAD**
The Grail calls only the pure. I thought I was alone in hearing it. But your lantern... it burns with the same light.

*He bows.*

**GALAHAD**
I would be honored to walk this path beside you.

---

### Merlin (Chapter 4, Stage 1)

*Reality cracks. Time stutters. An old man steps through a tear in the dream itself.*

*His eyes are young in an ancient face. Or ancient in a young face. It's hard to tell.*

**MERLIN**
Ah. There you are. I've been looking forward to this.

*He adjusts his spectacles.*

**MERLIN**
Or backward to it. Time is rather complicated when you live in the wrong direction.

**WART**
Merlyn!

**MERLIN**
*affectionately*
Wart. Still small. Still brave. Still terrible at your lessons.

*He turns to you.*

**MERLIN**
And you. The dreamer with the lantern. I've seen your future, you know. All the books you'll read. All the dreams you'll dream.

*He smiles.*

**MERLIN**
It's rather impressive.

**MC (internal)**
You know who I am?

**MERLIN**
*as if reading your thoughts*
I know who you will become. Which is more important than who you are.

*He produces a staff from nowhere.*

**MERLIN**
Now then. The war begins. Mordred marches. And I have put off teaching you the *really* important lessons long enough.

*His eyes twinkle.*

**MERLIN**
Let's see what you've learned.

## Part 8: Book 1 Ending (After The Awakening)

### Scene: The Shore of Avalon

*The light recedes. You stand on a silver shore. The water is perfectly still. Three queens wait in a black barge.*

*Arthur — the real Arthur, the grown Arthur — lies wounded among them.*

*Merlin stands beside you. Your heroes gather behind.*

**MERLIN**
You've done it. The dream survives. The story continues.

*Arthur's eyes find you. He smiles weakly.*

**ARTHUR**
So. You're the one. The dreamer who kept us alive.

*He reaches out. His hand is cold but strong.*

**ARTHUR**
Thank you. For believing in us. For fighting with us.

*He closes his eyes.*

**ARTHUR**
I go now to Avalon. To sleep until Britain needs me again.

*He looks at you one last time.*

**ARTHUR**
But you've proven something important: the story never truly ends. Not while someone still reads it. Still dreams it. Still believes.

*The barge begins to drift toward the mist.*

**BEDIVERE**
*throwing Excalibur into the lake*
Goodbye, my king. My friend.

*The sword arcs through the air. A hand — pale, beautiful — rises from the water to catch it.*

*Then both vanish beneath the surface.*

*The barge disappears into the mist. The dream begins to fade.*

**MERLIN**
Time to wake, young one. But don't worry.

*He winks.*

**MERLIN**
There are more books in the shop. More dreams to dream.

*The light takes you.*

---

### Scene: Bedroom, Morning

*You wake slowly. The room is familiar now — not as strange as before.*

*The book lies on your chest. The lantern glows faintly on the nightstand.*

*You open the book. The final page reads:*

**THE END**

*But as you watch, new words appear below:*

**...AND THE BEGINNING**

*You smile.*

*Downstairs, you hear your parents laughing. Outside, the sun is shining. Maybe this town isn't so bad after all.*

*But tonight...*

*Tonight, you'll visit the bookshop again.*

*There are more stories waiting.*

## Appendix: Writing Guidelines

### Tone
- Middle-grade appropriate but not condescending
- Emotional honesty over simplistic moralizing
- Wonder mixed with melancholy
- Humor through character, not jokes

### MC Voice
- Internal monologue only (MC rarely speaks aloud)
- Observant, thoughtful, slightly lonely
- Growing confidence over time
- Never sarcastic or cynical

### Dialogue Patterns
- Wart: Enthusiastic, earnest, self-deprecating
- Kay: Gruff, defensive, secretly caring
- Lancelot: Formal, melancholy, intense
- Morgana: Cryptic, bitter, occasionally vulnerable
- Bedivere: Stoic, loyal, grief-touched
- Merlin: Wise, whimsical, time-confused
- Escanor: Boastful, loud, surprisingly kind
- Galahad: Serene, gentle, uncomfortably perceptive

### Shopkeeper
- Never named (implied to be Merlin's other form? Another entity entirely? Left ambiguous)
- Knows more than he says
- Never explains the magic directly
- Always warm, never threatening
